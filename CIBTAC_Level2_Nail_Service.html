CIBTAC Level 2 Certificate in Nail Services - 156 hours (20 days) CIBTAC 二级美甲服务证书 - 156 小时（20 天）
overview 概述
The CIBTAC Level 2 Certificate in Nail Services is an internationally recognized qualification designed to equip learners with the theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and professional client-care standards required to perform high-quality manicure and pedicure treatments. CIBTAC 二级美甲服务证书是一项国际认可的资格，旨在为学员提供执行高质量美甲与足部护理所需的理论知识、实践技能以及专业的客户护理标准。
Recognition 认可
Upon successful completion, graduates of this programme can pursue their career as Makeup Artists. 成功完成后，本课程的毕业生可以从事化妆师职业。
Awarding Body 颁发机构
Awarded by Confederation of International Beauty Therapy & Cosmetology, UK (CIBTAC) 由英国国际美容治疗与美容学联合会（CIBTAC）颁发

Learning OutComes 学习成果
Upon successful completion of this qualification , learners will be able to: 成功完成本资格后，学员将能够：
  • Understand how to evaluate the promotion of products 理解如何评估产品推广
  • Know requirements for health, safety, hygiene and security practices and services 了解健康、安全、卫生与安保实践及服务的要求
  • Maintain health, safety, hygiene and security practices 维护健康、安全、卫生与安保实践
  • Understand the requirements for providing manicure 理解提供美甲的要求
  • Understand the requirements for Client care and communication and pedicure services 理解客户护理与沟通以及足部护理服务的要求
  • Communicate with Clients 与客户沟通
  • Understand anatomy and physiology relating to manicure and pedicure services 理解与美甲和足部护理服务相关的解剖学与生理学
  • Provide client care 提供客户护理
  • Promote and sell products and services 推广和销售产品与服务
  • Prepare for manicure and pedicure services 为美甲和足部护理服务做准备
  • Promote and sell beauty products 推广和销售美容产品
  • Provide manicure and pedicure services 提供美甲和足部护理服务
Graduation , Entry Requirments & Future 毕业、入学要求与未来
Graduation Requirments 毕业要求
Candidates must have 75% attendance in order to take the examination 考生必须达到 75% 的出勤率才能参加考试
Must obtain at least 60% to pass the exam 必须获得至少 60% 才能通过考试
Entry Requirments 入学要求
Minimum age: 16 years old (subject to local regulatory requirements) 最低年龄：16 岁（须符合当地监管要求）
Appropriate literacy and communication skills 具备适当的读写与沟通能力
Interest in beauty therapy, wellness or complementary therapy 对美容治疗、健康或辅助疗法有兴趣
Prior industry experience is beneficial but not essential 有行业经验为佳，但非必需

Qualification Structure 资格结构
To achieve the qualification, Learners must successfully complete four (4) mandatory units to achieve the qualification: 要获得该资格，学员必须成功完成四（4）个必修单元：
T109: Client care and communication in beauty related industries T109：美容相关行业的客户护理与沟通
T103: Promote and sell products and services to clients T103：向客户推广和销售产品与服务
T108: Follow health and safety in the salon T108：遵守沙龙健康与安全
P115: Provide manicure and pedicure services P115：提供美甲与足部护理服务
Get Certified 获得认证
Upon successful completion, Learners will be rewarded with certificate that verifies their knowledge, skills and reconising their competence to perform professional services and practise safely and effectivly within the their chosen area of study and complementary sectors 成功完成后，学员将获得证书，以验证他们的知识、技能，并认可他们在所选学习领域及辅助领域中安全有效地执行专业服务的能力。
