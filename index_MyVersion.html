Frequently Ask Questions 常见问题
Q. When is the commencement date for the course? 问：课程的开课日期是什么时候？
Please refer to our website for the updated course schedule. Alternatively, you may contact Beauty Valley at +65 6322 3088 for more information. 请参阅我们的网站以获取最新的课程时间表。或者，您可以致电 +65 6322 3088 联系 Beauty Valley 了解更多信息。
Q. What are the number of hours for a half-day and a full-day course? 问：半日课程和全日课程的时数分别是多少？
Half-day course (9am to 1pm or 2pm to 6pm)- 4 Hours per day 半日课程（上午9点至下午1点或下午2点至6点）- 每天4小时
Full-day course (9am to 6pm)- 8 Hours per day [1 hour lunch break] 全日课程（上午9点至下午6点）- 每天8小时 [1小时午休]
Q. Are the courses conducted in English or Chinese? 问：课程是以英语还是华语授课？
English and Chinese classes are available for all of our courses. 我们所有的课程都提供英语和华语班。
Q. What is the payment type for the course fees and examination fees? 问：课程费用和考试费用的支付方式是什么？
Payment of the course fee is to be made by cheque, cashier’s order, PayNow and IBanking. Miscellaneous fees can be paid through cheque, NETS, PayNow, or cash (in Singapore dollars) only. 课程费用的支付可通过支票、银行本票、PayNow和网上银行进行。杂费只能通过支票、NETS、PayNow或现金（仅限新加坡元）支付。
