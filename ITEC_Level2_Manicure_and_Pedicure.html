IUBT429-Level 2 Manicure and Pedicure IUBT429-二级美甲与足部护理
overview 概述
The main aim of the VTCT Skills (ITEC) Level 2 Certificate in Manicure and Pedicureis to enable learners to gain the necessary practical and theoretical skills in order to provide manicure and pedicure treatments to the general public and to gain employment in the beauty therapy industry. VTCT Skills (ITEC) 二级美甲与足部护理证书的主要目标是使学员获得必要的实践和理论知识，以便向公众提供美甲与足部护理服务，并在美容治疗行业就业。
Recognition 认可
Upon successful completion, graduates of this programme can pursue within the chosen career . 成功完成后，本课程的毕业生可以在所选职业领域内发展。
Awarding Body 颁发机构
Awarded by International Training and Examination Council (ITEC), United Kingdom 由英国国际培训与考试委员会（ITEC）颁发

Learning OutComes 学习成果
Upon successful completion of this qualification , learners will be able to: 成功完成本资格后，学员将能够：
  • Explain nail structure, conditions/ diseases and disorders 解释指甲结构、状况/疾病与失调
  • Perform care of the hands and feet 进行手部与足部护理
  • Perform nail care 进行指甲护理
  • Perform exfoliation 进行去角质
  • Perform nail/ hand massage 进行指甲/手部按摩
  • Apply nail painting 进行指甲涂色
