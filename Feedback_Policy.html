Complaint and Feedback Policy for Beauty Valley Academy Beauty Valley Academy 投诉与反馈政策
1. Scope 1. 范围
This policy applies to all learners, trainers, assessors, administrative staff, and any other individuals associated with Beauty Valley Academy. The purpose is to provide a transparent and systematic approach for handling complaints and feedback to ensure that incidents, concerns, or suggestions related to training, behavior, ethics, and safety are properly addressed. This policy covers issues arising within the Academy’s premises, during training hours, and related to both in-person and online training sessions. 本政策适用于所有学员、培训师、评估员、行政人员以及与 Beauty Valley Academy 相关的任何其他个人。其目的是提供透明且系统化的投诉与反馈处理方式，确保与培训、行为、道德和安全相关的事件、疑虑或建议得到妥善处理。本政策涵盖在学院场所内、培训时间内，以及与线下面授和线上培训课程相关的问题。
2. Objective 2. 目标
The objective of this policy is to: 本政策的目标是：
● Ensure prompt, fair, and transparent resolution of complaints. ● 确保投诉得到及时、公平和透明的解决。
● Maintain a safe, professional, and respectful learning environment. ● 维护安全、专业和相互尊重的学习环境。
● Protect the interests and well-being of all individuals at Beauty Valley Academy. ● 保护 Beauty Valley Academy 所有个人的利益和福祉。
● Support continuous improvement in the quality of training and administration services. ● 支持培训和行政服务质量的持续改进。
3. Types of Complaints 3. 投诉类型
The types of complaints covered under this policy include, but are not limited to: 本政策涵盖的投诉类型包括但不限于：
● Training Quality: Concerns about the quality of training, trainer punctuality, course content, or methodology. ● 培训质量：对培训质量、培训师守时情况、课程内容或教学方法的疑虑。
● Compliance and Ethics: Complaints about unethical behavior, policy violations, unauthorized conduct, or theft by trainers, assessors, or other staff. ● 合规与道德：关于培训师、评估员或其他员工的不道德行为、违反政策、未经授权的行为或盗窃的投诉。
● Harassment or Misconduct: Any form of harassment, physical or verbal abuse, discrimination, or inappropriate behavior during training sessions. ● 骚扰或不当行为：培训课程期间任何形式的骚扰、身体或言语虐待、歧视或不当行为。
● Health and Safety Incidents: Injuries, property damage, or any dangerous occurrences that happen within the Academy’s premises. ● 健康与安全事件：在学院场所内发生的伤害、财产损失或任何危险事件。
● Other Issues: Complaints regarding administrative support, delays, or actions taken by unauthorized personnel. ● 其他问题：关于行政支持、延误或未经授权人员采取行动的投诉。
4. Complaint Process 4. 投诉流程
Step 1: Submission of Complaint 步骤 1：提交投诉
● Complaints must be submitted within 48 hours of the incident. ● 投诉必须在事件发生后 48 小时内提交。
● Complainants should provide clear evidence and documentation to support their complaint, including witness statements if applicable. ● 投诉人应提供明确的证据和文件以支持其投诉，包括（如适用）证人陈述。
● The incident report should contain specific details, including: ● 事件报告应包含具体细节，包括：
o Name of the trainer or assessor involved. o 涉事培训师或评估员的姓名。
o Name of the administrative person in charge, if applicable. o 负责的行政人员姓名（如适用）。
o Venue or location of the incident. o 事件发生的场所或地点。
o Date and time of the incident. o 事件的日期和时间。
o Description of the incident (damage, injury, policy breach, harassment type, etc.). o 事件描述（损坏、受伤、违反政策、骚扰类型等）。
o Any available evidence, such as CCTV footage, signed incident report forms, or relevant documentation from the learner handbook. o 任何可用的证据，如闭路电视录像、已签署的事件报告表格，或学员手册中的相关文件。
o If the complaint involves unauthorized personnel or security breaches, details about the incident should be included. o 如果投诉涉及未经授权人员或安全漏洞，应包含有关该事件的详细信息。
Step 2: Acknowledgment and Initial Review 步骤 2：确认收到与初步审查
● Upon receipt, the administration will acknowledge the complaint within 24 hours and conduct a preliminary review. ● 收到投诉后，行政部门将在 24 小时内确认收到并进行初步审查。
● A designated Complaint Officer will be assigned to handle the case, ensuring confidentiality and impartiality. ● 将指定一名投诉专员负责处理该案件，确保保密性和公正性。
● The Complaint Officer will contact the complainant to clarify any additional information, if necessary. ● 如有必要，投诉专员将联系投诉人澄清任何补充信息。
Step 3: Investigation 步骤 3：调查
● The Academy will conduct an investigation within 5 working days of receiving the complaint, gathering relevant documents, evidence, and witness statements. ● 学院将在收到投诉后的 5 个工作日内进行调查，收集相关文件、证据和证人陈述。
● If serious allegations (such as harassment, assault, or severe policy violations) are involved, the investigation may extend up to 10 working days to ensure due diligence. ● 如果涉及严重指控（如骚扰、袭击或严重违反政策），调查可能会延长至 10 个工作日，以确保尽职调查。
● The investigation process may include: ● 调查过程可能包括：
o Reviewing CCTV footage, if available. o 审查闭路电视录像（如有）。
o Interviewing witnesses and involved parties. o 询问证人和涉事各方。
o Reviewing attendance logs, incident reports, and relevant sections of the learner handbook. o 审查出勤记录、事件报告以及学员手册的相关章节。
o Consulting with the administrative staff and trainers or assessors involved. o 与涉事的行政人员及培训师或评估员进行协商。
Step 4: Resolution and Action 步骤 4：解决与行动
● Based on the findings, the Academy will determine the appropriate action within 7 working days of completing the investigation. Possible actions may include: ● 根据调查结果，学院将在完成调查后的 7 个工作日内确定适当的行动。可能的行动包括：
o Corrective Action: Retraining, additional support, or policy reinforcement for trainers or staff. o 纠正措施：对培训师或员工进行再培训、额外支持或政策强化。
o Disciplinary Action: Warnings, suspension, or termination for trainers, assessors, or staff found in breach of Academy policies. o 纪律处分：对被发现违反学院政策的培训师、评估员或员工给予警告、停职或解雇。
o Health and Safety Action: Repair of property, review of safety procedures, or additional safety training if needed. o 健康与安全措施：维修财产、审查安全程序，或（如有需要）进行额外的安全培训。
o Feedback to the Complainant: The complainant will receive a written summary of the findings and any steps taken as a result. o 向投诉人反馈：投诉人将收到调查结果及所采取任何措施的书面摘要。
Step 5: Appeal (If Applicable) 步骤 5：申诉（如适用）
● If the complainant is not satisfied with the resolution, they may file an appeal within 5 working days of receiving the resolution notification. ● 如果投诉人对处理结果不满意，可在收到处理结果通知后的 5 个工作日内提出申诉。
● An independent Appeals Officer will review the case and make a final determination within 10 working days. ● 独立的申诉专员将在 10 个工作日内审查该案件并作出最终裁定。
5. Duration of Process 5. 流程时长
● Initial Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours of receiving the complaint. ● 初步确认：在收到投诉后的 24 小时内。
● Investigation and Resolution: Typically within 10-15 working days from the date of complaint receipt. ● 调查与解决：通常在收到投诉之日起 10-15 个工作日内。
● Appeal Review: Completed within 10 working days of receiving the appeal. ● 申诉审查：在收到申诉后的 10 个工作日内完成。
6. Documentation and Record Keeping 6. 文件与记录保存
● All complaints, incident reports, and investigation records will be securely stored for audit and compliance purposes. ● 所有投诉、事件报告和调查记录将被安全保存，以供审计和合规之用。
● Records are maintained according to PDPA standards, ensuring confidentiality and restricted access. ● 记录按照 PDPA 标准保存，确保保密性和受限访问。
7. Confidentiality and Data Protection 7. 保密性与数据保护
Beauty Valley Academy is committed to handling all complaints and feedback confidentially. Only individuals directly involved in the investigation process will have access to relevant details, and all data will be stored securely. Beauty Valley Academy 致力于以保密方式处理所有投诉和反馈。只有直接参与调查过程的人员才能访问相关细节，所有数据都将被安全保存。
8. Code of Conduct and Expectations 8. 行为准则与期望
All staff, trainers, and learners are expected to adhere to the Academy’s Code of Conduct, which includes: 所有员工、培训师和学员都应遵守学院的行为准则，其中包括：
● Professional behavior, punctuality, and respect. ● 专业行为、守时和尊重。
● Adherence to ethical standards and prevention of policy violations. ● 遵守道德标准并防止违反政策。
● No tolerance for harassment, unauthorized behavior, or security breaches. ● 对骚扰、未经授权的行为或安全漏洞零容忍。
9. Review and Continuous Improvement 9. 审查与持续改进
This policy will be reviewed annually to incorporate any necessary improvements based on feedback and regulatory updates. Feedback from complaint resolution outcomes will also be used to enhance Academy policies and practices. 本政策将每年审查一次，以根据反馈和监管更新纳入任何必要的改进。投诉解决结果的反馈也将用于改进学院的政策和实践。
