Scope and Procedure for Investigating Malpractice and Maladministration 调查渎职与行政失当的范围与程序
1. Scope 1. 范围
This procedure applies to all suspected instances of malpractice and maladministration associated with course delivery at Beauty Valley Academy. It covers pre- and post-course administration, ensures comprehensive investigation within a 5-working-day period, and emphasizes preventive training and documentation to minimize future incidents. 本程序适用于与 Beauty Valley Academy 课程交付相关的所有涉嫌渎职和行政失当的情况。它涵盖课程前和课程后的行政管理，确保在 5 个工作日内进行全面调查，并强调预防性培训和文件记录，以尽量减少未来事件的发生。
2. Objectives 2. 目标
● To establish a standardized approach for handling malpractice and maladministration. ● 建立处理渎职和行政失当的标准化方法。
● To ensure investigations are conducted timely and fairly. ● 确保调查及时且公平地进行。
● To maintain transparency, integrity, and accountability in managing cases. ● 在案件管理中保持透明、诚信和问责。
● To document findings for future reference, case study learning, and continuous improvement. ● 记录调查结果，以供未来参考、案例学习和持续改进。
3. Procedure 3. 程序
3.1 Pre-Administration (Initial Steps) 3.1 行政前阶段（初始步骤）
1. Identification of Incident: Any suspected malpractice or maladministration (e.g., falsification of records, course standard breaches, or unethical practices) should be immediately reported to the course administrator. 1. 事件识别：任何涉嫌渎职或行政失当的行为（例如伪造记录、违反课程标准或不道德行为）应立即向课程管理员报告。
2. Notification: Within 1 working day of receiving a report, the course administrator notifies the designated Investigation Committee. 2. 通知：在收到报告后的 1 个工作日内，课程管理员通知指定的调查委员会。
3. Assigning the Investigation Committee: A committee comprising 3-5 members, including representatives from top management, HR, and an independent third-party reviewer (if applicable), will be formed. 3. 任命调查委员会：将成立一个由 3-5 名成员组成的委员会，包括高层管理人员、人力资源部代表以及（如适用）独立的第三方审查员。
4. Initial Review: The committee conducts an initial assessment to verify the details and scope of the incident, determining whether further investigation is warranted. 4. 初步审查：委员会进行初步评估，以核实事件的细节和范围，确定是否需要进一步调查。
3.2 Investigation (Day 1 – Day 4) 3.2 调查（第 1 天至第 4 天）
1. Collection of Evidence: 1. 收集证据：
o The Investigation Committee gathers relevant documentation, interviews witnesses, and reviews course records and communications. o 调查委员会收集相关文件、询问证人，并审查课程记录和通信。
o Each party involved is interviewed independently to ensure objective information gathering. o 对涉及的每一方进行独立询问，以确保客观地收集信息。
2. Detailed Analysis: 2. 详细分析：
o Evidence is analyzed to determine the root cause, individuals involved, and the extent of malpractice or maladministration. o 分析证据以确定根本原因、涉及人员以及渎职或行政失当的程度。
o The committee assesses whether the incident was intentional, due to lack of awareness, or the result of procedural oversight. o 委员会评估该事件是故意行为、因缺乏意识还是程序疏忽所致。
3. Interim Report (End of Day 4): 3. 中期报告（第 4 天结束时）：
o The committee compiles an interim report summarizing findings, the nature of the incident, evidence gathered, and immediate corrective actions required. o 委员会编写中期报告，总结调查结果、事件性质、收集的证据以及需要立即采取的纠正措施。
3.3 Post-Administration (Day 5 – Report Finalization) 3.3 行政后阶段（第 5 天 – 报告定稿）
1. Final Report 1. 最终报告
o By Day 5, the committee finalizes a report detailing the investigation, findings, conclusions, and recommendations. o 到第 5 天，委员会完成一份详细说明调查、结果、结论和建议的报告。
o The report should include: o 报告应包括：
▪ Incident summary and background context. ▪ 事件摘要和背景情况。
▪ Evidence analysis. ▪ 证据分析。
▪ Identified causes and parties involved. ▪ 查明的原因和涉及的各方。
▪ Recommendations for preventive actions and procedural improvements. ▪ 预防措施和程序改进的建议。
2. Presentation and Decision: 2. 陈述与决定：
o The final report is presented to top management, who decides on any disciplinary actions, corrective measures, or procedural changes. o 最终报告提交给高层管理人员，由其决定任何纪律处分、纠正措施或程序变更。
o Approved actions are assigned for implementation based on the committee’s recommendations. o 根据委员会的建议，批准的行动被指派执行。
3.4 Documentation and Case Closure 3.4 文件记录与案件结案
1. Implementation of Actions: 1. 行动的实施：
o Corrective actions and improvements are implemented within the specified time frame by top management. o 高层管理人员在规定的时间范围内实施纠正措施和改进。
2. Case Documentation: 2. 案件文件记录：
o All case-related documents (reports, meeting notes, emails) are securely stored in compliance with data protection policies. o 所有与案件相关的文件（报告、会议记录、电子邮件）均按照数据保护政策安全保存。
o Records are maintained for future auditing, quality assurance, and reference. o 记录保存以供未来审计、质量保证和参考。
3. Case Closure: 3. 案件结案：
o The case is officially closed once all recommended actions are complete. o 一旦所有建议的行动完成，案件即正式结案。
o A summary of the case and lessons learned is shared with relevant stakeholders and documented in the academy’s annual review report. o 案件摘要和经验教训将与相关利益相关者分享，并记录在学院的年度审查报告中。
4. Preventive Measures and Training 4. 预防措施与培训
4.1 Prevention Procedures 4.1 预防程序
1. Clear Code of Conduct: A detailed code of conduct outlining acceptable behavior, ethical standards, and reporting procedures is disseminated to all staff and trainers. 1. 明确的行为准则：向所有员工和培训师分发详细的行为准则，概述可接受的行为、道德标准和报告程序。
2. Quality Checks: Regular audits and quality checks of course administration processes to ensure compliance with established standards. 2. 质量检查：定期对课程管理流程进行审计和质量检查，以确保符合既定标准。
3. Real-Time Monitoring: Use of real-time monitoring systems during course delivery to identify and address any issues immediately. 3. 实时监控：在课程交付期间使用实时监控系统，立即识别和解决任何问题。
4.2 Training Program 4.2 培训计划
1. Training Audience: 1. 培训对象：
o All Staff: Awareness of reporting channels, compliance procedures, and ethical standards. o 所有员工：了解报告渠道、合规程序和道德标准。
o Trainers and Course Developers: Focus on maintaining high standards, understanding malpractice and maladministration, and following procedures. o 培训师和课程开发者：专注于保持高标准、理解渎职和行政失当以及遵循程序。
o Investigation Committee Members: Advanced training on investigation procedures, root cause analysis, and report preparation. o 调查委员会成员：关于调查程序、根本原因分析和报告准备的高级培训。
2. Training Frequency: 2. 培训频率：
o Initial Training: Conducted during onboarding for new staff and trainers. o 初始培训：在新员工和培训师入职期间进行。
o Annual Refresher: All staff must attend annual training on ethical standards, prevention, and reporting of malpractice. o 年度进修：所有员工必须参加关于道德标准、预防和报告渎职的年度培训。
o Quarterly Workshops: Investigation Committee members and department heads attend quarterly workshops focused on case study reviews and continuous improvement. o 季度研讨会：调查委员会成员和部门主管参加季度研讨会，重点关注案例研究审查和持续改进。
4.3 Documentation as Lessons Learned and Case Studies 4.3 作为经验教训和案例研究的文件记录
1. Lessons Learned Log: 1. 经验教训日志：
o Each case of malpractice or maladministration is reviewed and documented in a “Lessons Learned Log,” highlighting the root causes, corrective actions, and preventive measures. o 每一起渎职或行政失当案件都会在“经验教训日志”中进行审查和记录，突出根本原因、纠正措施和预防措施。
o The log is accessible to relevant staff for reference and applied to ongoing training sessions. o 该日志可供相关员工参考，并应用于持续进行的培训课程中。
2. Case Studies for Training: 2. 培训案例研究：
o Notable cases are anonymized and turned into case studies to be used in training sessions and workshops. o 将值得注意的案件匿名化并转化为案例研究，用于培训课程和研讨会。
o Case studies focus on identifying risks, understanding the impact of malpractice, and recognizing best practices in prevention and response. o 案例研究侧重于识别风险、理解渎职的影响，以及认可预防和应对的最佳实践。
3. Continuous Improvement Feedback Loop: 3. 持续改进反馈循环：
o Feedback from lessons learned and case studies is used to update the code of conduct, SOPs, and training materials to continuously enhance the prevention of malpractice and maladministration. o 来自经验教训和案例研究的反馈用于更新行为准则、标准操作程序和培训材料，以持续加强渎职和行政失当的预防。
5. Quality Assurance and Review 5. 质量保证与审查
● Annual Review: The procedures for malpractice and maladministration prevention, investigation, and reporting are reviewed annually to incorporate any regulatory changes or organizational improvements. ● 年度审查：每年审查渎职和行政失当的预防、调查和报告程序，以纳入任何法规变更或组织改进。
● Audit and Feedback: Regular audits and feedback sessions help identify any gaps in the current process, ensuring a proactive approach to maintaining integrity and quality. ● 审计与反馈：定期审计和反馈会议有助于识别当前流程中的任何差距，确保以积极主动的方式维护诚信和质量。
This SOP ensures a comprehensive approach to managing and preventing malpractice and maladministration, emphasizing fair investigation, training, and continuous improvement. 本标准操作程序确保以全面的方法管理和预防渎职与行政失当，强调公平调查、培训和持续改进。
