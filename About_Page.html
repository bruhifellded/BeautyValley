About Us
Welcome to Beauty Valley !
At Beauty Training Institute, we are dedicated to shaping the future of the beauty industry through excellence in education and professional empowerment.
Company BackGround
Beauty Valley Pte Ltd, located in the vibrant heart of Chinatown, is a leading beauty education and service provider in Singapore. With a global vision and a commitment to excellence, we offer comprehensive beauty training programs and a range of innovative brand services. Our mission is to cultivate future professionals in the beauty industry while providing transformative beauty experiences for clients worldwide.
Our Mission
We focus on offering high-quality training to students and helping customers achieve transformative beauty experiences.
Educational Philosophy
With our philosophy of “learning with benchmarks, doing with standards, and pursuing with directions,” we ensure a balance of theory and practice in beauty training.
Strong Faculty
Our faculty consists of industry veterans and renowned beauty experts from both Singapore and abroad. Their expertise and cutting-edge teaching methods ensure that students receive the most current and relevant knowledge, enabling them to explore the infinite possibilities of beauty.
diversified integration
A professional brand that integrates nail and eyelash art, beauty, and tattoo art, with three exquisite stores. We select high-quality beauty products, focus on problem skin management, combine exquisite technology with scientific skin care concepts, and bring more beautiful and meticulous skin.
As a rising star in the SPA field, No.1 Store focuses on physical health care and supplemented by beauty and skin care. Through the combination of professional massage techniques and natural herbal products, it brings customers comprehensive relaxation and restoration of body and mind.
