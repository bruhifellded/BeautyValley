About Us 关于我们
Welcome to Beauty Valley ! 欢迎来到 Beauty Valley！
At Beauty Training Institute, we are dedicated to shaping the future of the beauty industry through excellence in education and professional empowerment. 在美容培训学院，我们致力于通过卓越的教育和专业赋能来塑造美容行业的未来。
Company BackGround 公司背景
Beauty Valley Pte Ltd, located in the vibrant heart of Chinatown, is a leading beauty education and service provider in Singapore. With a global vision and a commitment to excellence, we offer comprehensive beauty training programs and a range of innovative brand services. Our mission is to cultivate future professionals in the beauty industry while providing transformative beauty experiences for clients worldwide. Beauty Valley Pte Ltd 位于充满活力的牛车水中心，是新加坡领先的美容教育与服务提供商。凭借全球视野和对卓越的承诺，我们提供全面的美容培训课程以及一系列创新品牌服务。我们的使命是培养美容行业的未来专业人才，同时为全球客户提供蜕变式的美容体验。
Our Mission 我们的使命
We focus on offering high-quality training to students and helping customers achieve transformative beauty experiences. 我们专注于为学生提供高质量培训，并帮助客户获得蜕变式的美容体验。
Educational Philosophy 教育理念
With our philosophy of “learning with benchmarks, doing with standards, and pursuing with directions,” we ensure a balance of theory and practice in beauty training. 我们秉持“学有标杆、做有标准、追有方向”的理念，确保美容培训中理论与实践并重。
Strong Faculty 强大的师资
Our faculty consists of industry veterans and renowned beauty experts from both Singapore and abroad. Their expertise and cutting-edge teaching methods ensure that students receive the most current and relevant knowledge, enabling them to explore the infinite possibilities of beauty. 我们的师资团队由来自新加坡及海外的行业资深人士和知名美容专家组成。他们的专业知识和前沿教学方法确保学生获得最新、最相关的知识，使他们能够探索美容的无限可能。
diversified integration 多元化融合
A professional brand that integrates nail and eyelash art, beauty, and tattoo art, with three exquisite stores. We select high-quality beauty products, focus on problem skin management, combine exquisite technology with scientific skin care concepts, and bring more beautiful and meticulous skin. 一个融合美甲美睫、美容与纹绣艺术的专业品牌，拥有三家精致门店。我们精选高品质美容产品，专注于问题肌肤管理，将精湛技术与科学护肤理念相结合，带来更美丽、更细致的肌肤。
As a rising star in the SPA field, No.1 Store focuses on physical health care and supplemented by beauty and skin care. Through the combination of professional massage techniques and natural herbal products, it brings customers comprehensive relaxation and restoration of body and mind. 作为SPA领域的新星，第一门店以身体健康护理为主，辅以美容与护肤。通过专业按摩手法与天然草本产品的结合，为顾客带来身心的全面放松与恢复。
