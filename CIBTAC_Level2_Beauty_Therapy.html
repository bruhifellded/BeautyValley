CIBTAC Level 2 Diploma in Beauty Therapy Services - 336 hours (42 days)
overview
The CIBTAC Level 2 Diploma in Beauty Therapy Services is an internationally recognized qualification designed to equip aspiring beauty professionals with essential theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and professional standards required for the beauty industry.
Recognition
Upon successful completion, graduates of this programme can pursue their career as Makeup Artists.
Awarding Body
Awarded by Confederation of International Beauty Therapy & Cosmetology, UK (CIBTAC)

Learning OutComes
Upon successful completion of this qualification , learners will be able to:
  • Comply with salon health and safety regulations
  • Conduct professional client consultations and treatments
  • Manage salon frontOdesk operations
  • Deliver facial skin care services
  • Provide eyelash and eyebrow treatments
  • Perform hairDremoval waxing services
  • Offer hand and foot care (manicure & pedicure)
  • Apply professional makeup techniques
Graduation , Entry Requirments & Future
Graduation Requirments
Candidates must have 75% attendance in order to take the examination
Must obtain at least 60% to pass the exam
Entry Requirments
Minimum age: 16 years old (subject to local regulatory requirements)
Appropriate literacy and communication skills
Interest in beauty therapy, wellness or complementary therapy
Prior industry experience is beneficial but not essential

Assessment may include:
· Practical treatment assessments
· Written assignments
· Portfolio of evidence

Qualification Structure
To achieve the qualification, Learners must successfully complete all eight (8) mandatory units.
T108: Follow health and safety in the salon
T109: Client care and communication in beauty
T110: Salon reception duties
P110: Provide facial skincare
P113: Provide eyelash and eyebrow services
P114: Provide waxing services
P115: Provide manicure and pedicure services
P116: Apply make-up
Get Certified
Upon successful completion, Learners will be rewarded with certificate that verifies their knowledge, skills and reconising their competence to perform professional services and practise safely and effectivly within the their chosen area of study and complementary sectors
