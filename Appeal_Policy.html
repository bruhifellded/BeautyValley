Appeal Policy 申诉政策
1. Scope/Purpose 1. 范围/目的
Enhanced Beauty Valley Academy Appeal Procedure and Scope 增强的 Beauty Valley Academy 申诉程序与范围
At Beauty Valley Academy, we strive to maintain transparency, fairness, and integrity throughout the assessment process. Our appeal procedure provides candidates with an opportunity to request a re-assessment should they receive a "Not Yet Competent" (NYC) result. The following outlines the steps, requirements, and candidate responsibilities involved in filing an appeal. 在 Beauty Valley Academy，我们致力于在整个评估过程中保持透明、公平和诚信。我们的申诉程序为考生提供了在收到“尚未胜任”（NYC）结果时申请重新评估的机会。以下概述了提出申诉所涉及的步骤、要求和考生责任。
Scope: 范围：
This procedure applies to all candidates who have been awarded a "Not Yet Competent" (NYC) result in any assessment conducted by Beauty Valley Academy. Eligible candidates can apply for a re-test within the specified time frame. 本程序适用于在 Beauty Valley Academy 进行的任何评估中获得“尚未胜任”（NYC）结果的所有考生。符合条件的考生可在规定时间内申请补考。
Eligibility: 资格：
1. Time Frame: Candidates must submit an appeal within 24 hours of receiving their NYC result. 1. 时间范围：考生必须在收到 NYC 成绩后的 24 小时内提交申诉。
2. Pre-requisites: Candidates must meet the pre-course entry requirements and the minimum qualifications to be eligible for further appeals. 2. 先决条件：考生必须满足课程入学要求及最低资格，才有资格进一步申诉。
2. Appeal Procedure 2. 申诉程序
Step 1: Submit Appeal Request 步骤 1：提交申诉请求
○ Candidates must approach the administrative office within 24 hours of receiving their NYC result to request an appeal. ○ 考生必须在收到 NYC 成绩后的 24 小时内联系行政办公室提出申诉请求。
○ The candidate must fill out an appeal form, including a clear explanation for the appeal, along with any supporting evidence to justify the request. ○ 考生必须填写申诉表格，包括明确的申诉理由以及任何支持该请求的证据。
○ An appeal fee of $120 must be paid, covering the re-assessment setup and the assessor's engagement fee. ○ 必须支付 120 美元的申诉费，用于重新评估的设置及评估员的聘用费。
○ Candidates must acknowledge that the re-assessment will be conducted with a different set of questions (Set B). ○ 考生必须确认重新评估将使用不同的试题集（B 套）进行。
Step 2: Candidate Acknowledgement and Coaching Option 步骤 2：考生确认与辅导选项
○ Candidates are required to accept the results and feedback provided by the assessor during the initial assessment. ○ 考生必须接受评估员在初始评估期间提供的结果和反馈。
○ Candidates must ensure that an oral clarification has been conducted with the assessor, confirming they fully understand the performance gaps and competency criteria. ○ 考生必须确保已与评估员进行口头澄清，确认他们完全理解表现差距和能力标准。
○ Candidates have the option to request further coaching or assistance if they feel additional support is needed before the re-assessment. ○ 如果考生认为在重新评估前需要额外支持，可以选择请求进一步辅导或协助。
○ The pre-assessment briefing, procedure, and criteria must be acknowledged by the candidate before proceeding with the appeal. ○ 在继续进行申诉之前，考生必须确认已了解评估前简报、程序和标准。
Step 3: Review by Exam Board 步骤 3：考试委员会审查
○ The exam board will review the appeal request, including the supporting evidence, within 3 working days. ○ 考试委员会将在 3 个工作日内审查申诉请求，包括支持证据。
○ The review will consider reports from both the assessor and the administrative office, ensuring a thorough evaluation of the candidate's performance. ○ 审查将考虑评估员和行政办公室的报告，确保对考生的表现进行全面评估。
○ If the appeal is successful, a re-assessment will be scheduled, and the candidate will proceed with Set B of the assessment. If the appeal is denied, the board will provide a clear rationale for the decision. ○ 如果申诉成功，将安排重新评估，考生将进行 B 套评估。如果申诉被拒绝，委员会将提供明确的决定理由。
Step 4: Re-assessment (If Appeal is Granted) 步骤 4：重新评估（若申诉获批）
○ Upon approval of the appeal, the re-assessment will be scheduled. The candidate must attend the re-assessment as instructed and adhere to all procedures. ○ 申诉获批后，将安排重新评估。考生必须按照指示参加重新评估并遵守所有程序。
○ The re-assessment will involve a new assessment set (Set B), and candidates must ensure they are prepared for this evaluation. ○ 重新评估将涉及新的评估套件（B 套），考生必须确保为此评估做好准备。
Step 5: Limitations on Appeals 步骤 5：申诉限制
○ Candidates are only permitted two appeals. If the result of the second appeal is still "Not Yet Competent," the candidate must re-enroll in the full course without any subsidy and settle the full course fees. ○ 考生仅允许申诉两次。如果第二次申诉的结果仍为“尚未胜任”，考生必须重新报名参加完整课程，且不享受任何补贴，并结清全部课程费用。
○ No further appeals will be accepted beyond the second attempt. ○ 第二次尝试之后将不再接受任何进一步申诉。
3. Responsibilities 3. 责任
Candidate: 考生：
○ Submit the appeal request within 24 hours of receiving the NYC result. ○ 在收到 NYC 成绩后的 24 小时内提交申诉请求。
○ Acknowledge the assessment feedback and performance gaps provided by the assessor. ○ 确认评估员提供的评估反馈和表现差距。
○ Ensure the oral clarification and pre-assessment briefing have been conducted. ○ 确保已进行口头澄清和评估前简报。
○ Pay the $120 appeal fee and understand that the re-assessment will use a new question set (Set B). ○ 支付 120 美元的申诉费，并了解重新评估将使用新的试题集（B 套）。
○ Seek additional coaching or support if required. ○ 如有需要，寻求额外的辅导或支持。
○ Abide by the two-appeal limit and understand that re-enrollment in the course is required if both appeals are unsuccessful. ○ 遵守两次申诉限制，并了解如果两次申诉均未成功，则需要重新报名参加课程。
Administrative Office: 行政办公室：
○ Provide the appeal form and offer guidance on the appeal process. ○ 提供申诉表格并就申诉流程提供指导。
○ Collect and process the appeal form and fee. ○ 收集并处理申诉表格和费用。
○ Submit a detailed report of the candidate's performance and any relevant information from the first assessment. ○ 提交考生表现的详细报告以及第一次评估中的任何相关信息。
Assessor: 评估员：
○ Provide a comprehensive report on the candidate's performance, including feedback, assessment details, and any oral clarification provided to the candidate. ○ 提供关于考生表现的全面报告，包括反馈、评估细节以及向考生提供的任何口头澄清。
○ Submit the report to the exam board for consideration. ○ 将报告提交给考试委员会审议。
Exam Board: 考试委员会：
○ Review the appeal, evidence, and reports from both the assessor and the administrative office. ○ 审查申诉、证据以及来自评估员和行政办公室的报告。
○ Make a decision within 3 working days and notify the candidate of the outcome. ○ 在 3 个工作日内做出决定，并将结果通知考生。
○ Provide a clear explanation if the appeal is denied. ○ 如果申诉被拒绝，提供明确的解释。
4. Final Decision 4. 最终决定
The decision made by the exam board is final. Candidates are required to accept the outcome of the appeal process, whether granted or denied. In cases where further clarification or assistance is needed, candidates may approach the administrative office for additional guidance. 考试委员会做出的决定为最终决定。考生必须接受申诉过程的结果，无论获批还是被拒。如需进一步澄清或协助，考生可联系行政办公室获取额外指导。
