UTAP_Basic_Hair_&_Make_Up - 24 hours UTAP_基础发型与化妆 - 24 小时
overview 概述
This course is to provide participants with foundational skills in hairstyling and makeup application, focusing on techniques, tools, and creativity. By the end of the course, students will be equipped with the knowledge to create everyday, professional, and special occasion looks, as well as understanding proper hygiene and product use. 本课程旨在为学员提供发型设计和化妆应用的基础技能，重点关注技巧、工具和创意。课程结束时，学员将掌握打造日常、专业和特殊场合造型的知识，并了解正确的卫生和产品使用。
Recognition 认可
Upon successful completion, graduates of this programme can pursue their career as Makeup Artists. 成功完成后，本课程的毕业生可以从事化妆师职业。
Objective 目标
Learner will be able to be learn and perform within the their chosen area of study 学员将能够在所选学习领域内学习和实践。
